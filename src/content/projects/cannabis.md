## Grown like wheat, tiered like ores

Twelve strains come in three tiers. Tier I seeds drop rarely from grass. Tier II and Tier III don't drop anywhere: you breed them. Each plant grows through eight stages, ripe plants shed drifting flecks so you know when to harvest, and bees speed them up while bone meal won't.

| Tier | Strains | Effects | How to get it |
| ---- | ------- | ------- | ------------- |
| I | Cocoa Bean Gelato, Creeper Crunch, Glowstone Gold, Magma Cream OG | Regeneration I, Resistance I | Rare drop from grass |
| II | Lapis OG, Nether Wart Punch, Enderman Haze, Cherry Blossom Kush | Regeneration II, Resistance II | Cross-breed Tier I strains |
| III | Ender Dragon Breath, Diamond Ore OG, Obsidian OG, Ghast Gas | Regeneration III, Resistance II | Cross-breed Tier II strains |

Every strain also gives Night Vision.

## Cross-breed at the Mixing Table

Load a seed of one strain, a bud of another from the same tier and a catalyst, then start the cross. Every pair makes something, but nothing tells you what. The table's Research Journal records each cross you discover, and a click refills the table from your inventory.

## Roll it, pack it, cook it

Roll joints and blunts, pack a Thai Stick or a glass bong, brew golden concentrates and dab them with a Nectar Collector, or cook edibles in a furnace with Canna-Butter. Everything uses vanilla-style recipes, Minecraft's own sounds, and smoke that other players can see.

## Built for servers

Everything runs on the server and the mod sends no custom network packets. The mod must be installed on the server and on every player's game, all on the same version. Worlds, items and planted crops carry over between versions.

## Official, signed downloads

Downloads only come from the project's GitHub Releases. Each release lists SHA-256 checksums, and every jar is signed by the author. The included Windows updater installs only a correctly signed jar and keeps a backup of your old one.
